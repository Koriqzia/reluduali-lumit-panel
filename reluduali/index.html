What this extension sends, and nothing else

  1. Signing in: your name and password, once.
  2. About once a minute, while your window is open: how long you edited and rendered in Lumit, what that charged, and a fingerprint of your edit record so far.
  3. When a render finishes: its fingerprint and size. Never the file or its name.
  4. When you hand in: the links you type, and your edit record: the name and time of each step you made in the contest project, as Lumit names them (“Set keyframe”). Never what the step did, and nothing from another project.
  5. With a partner: the invite link to your shared Lumit project, for your partner alone.

All of it goes through Lumit to reluduali.com, and nowhere else. This panel keeps a plain list of every message it sent: Signed in as…, at the bottom of the menu, once you are in.
